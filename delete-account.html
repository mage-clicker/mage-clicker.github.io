Delete your Mage Clicker account

Mage Clicker for Android, made by Tobias Meyer

Every player of Mage Clicker has an anonymous account: a random player ID with your mage name, your progress, your score and your guild seat. You can delete it, and everything stored under it, in two ways.

In the game (deleted at once)

  1. Open Mage Clicker.
  2. Tap the gear at the top right of the Workshop to open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm twice.

Your account is deleted immediately, on your phone and online. The game then starts over with a new, empty account.

By email (if you no longer have the game)

  1. Write to mage.clicker0@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account".
  2. Include your mage name and, if you still have it, your transfer code (the game shows it under Settings → Transfer code).

Accounts are anonymous, so I may ask you a question or two about the account (for example its circle or its guild) to make sure the request comes from its owner. The account is deleted within 30 days, and you get a reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your player ID and sign-in
  • Your mage name and your leaderboard score
  • The online backup of your progress and your gem balance
  • Your transfer code and the record of earlier player IDs that moved onto your account
  • Your guild membership, raid strikes and quest progress (your seat is given up as if you had left; a guild whose last member leaves is deleted)
  • Reports you made, and reports others made about your mage name
  • Error reports sent by your game

What is kept

  • Entries in your guild's activity feed and the quests you finished stay in the guild, but without your name or player ID. The feed itself only reaches back 14 days.
  • Reports that other players made about the name of a guild stay in the review list with that guild's name, but with nothing about you.
  • Backups of the database kept by the hosting provider expire on their own, within 30 days at the latest.

Nothing else is kept. Removing only the app from your phone deletes the data on the phone but not the online account, so use one of the two ways above.
