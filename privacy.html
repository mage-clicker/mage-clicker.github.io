Privacy policy

Mage Clicker for Android · Last updated: November 22, 2026

Mage Clicker ("the game") is made by Tobias Meyer, a private developer ("I", "me"). This page says what the game stores about you, why, for how long, and how you get it deleted.

In short

  • You play under a random player ID and the mage name you choose. The game never asks for your real name, email address or phone number.
  • This version has no ads, no purchases and no tracking.
  • Your progress is backed up to a database in the European Union so it survives a new phone.
  • You can delete everything yourself: Settings → Delete account.

What the game stores, and why

An anonymous player account

The first time the game goes online, its backend creates an account with a random player ID. No name, email address or phone number is asked for or stored. Your progress, your score and your guild seat are kept under this ID.

Your mage name

If you choose a mage name, it is public: other players see it on the leaderboard and in your guild. Choose a name that does not identify you. You can change it in Settings at any time.

Your progress and your score

Your game progress (mana, upgrades, gear, talents, achievements and so on) is saved on your device and backed up to the game's database, so that it survives a reinstall and so that your score can stand on the leaderboard. Together with the backup the database keeps your gem balance and a random ID of the installation it came from, which tells your devices apart. Only your own account can read your backup. Other players see only your mage name, your circle and your score.

Transfer code

So that you can move your progress to a new phone, or get it back after reinstalling the game, your account has a transfer code (Settings → Transfer code). The code is kept on your device; the database stores only a scrambled (hashed) form of it, which cannot be turned back into the code. When the code is entered on another device, your progress, gem balance, mage name, score and guild membership move to that device's player ID, the account gets a new code, and the old player ID can no longer save online. A record linking the old player ID to the new one is kept, so that the old device cannot keep playing online with a copy. Anyone who has your code can take over your progress, so keep it private.

Guilds

If you found or join a guild, the database stores your membership (which guild, your role, when you joined and when you were last active), your circle and deepest dungeon level (they set your raid strength), your raid strikes, the quests you take and finish, and the preset cheers you give. The other members of your guild can see your mage name, role, circle, when you were last active and this week's raid damage and quests, plus a short activity feed of the guild's events. Guild names and banners are public in the guild directory. There is no free-text chat: members can only send preset cheers.

Reports

If you report a guild name or another player's mage name, the report is stored (your player ID, the guild or player you reported, and the name at that time) so that I can review it.

Error reports

When the game runs into an error, it sends a short technical report to the database: the error message and where in the code it happened, the game version, which screen was open, and your device's browser identification (phone model, Android version and WebView version). The report is linked to your player ID so that repeats can be told apart, is used only to fix bugs, and is deleted after 30 days.

Connection data

Whenever the game talks to its backend, the backend provider has to process your device's IP address to answer the request, and keeps it in its server logs for a short time for security and troubleshooting. I do not use these logs to identify you.

On your device

The game keeps your save, your settings, your player ID's sign-in and your transfer code in the app's own storage on your device. Uninstalling the game removes them from the device. If backups are switched on in your phone's settings, Android may include this storage in the device backup of your own Google account; that copy belongs to your Google account, not to the game.

What the game does not collect

Your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, files or browsing history.

This version of the game contains no ads, no in-app purchases, no advertising ID and no analytics or tracking tools. Should ads or purchases be added in a later version, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Who receives your data

  • Supabase, Inc. runs the game's database and sign-in service on my behalf, as a processor under a data processing agreement. The data is stored in the European Union (Ireland). Supabase is based in the United States; where personal data could be accessed from outside the EU, this is covered by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses in that agreement. See supabase.com/privacy.
  • Other players see your mage name, circle and score on the leaderboard and, inside your guild, the details listed under "Guilds".

Your data is not sold, and it is not shared with advertisers or with anyone else.

Legal bases

Where the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies:

  • Running the online features you use (your account, the backup of your progress, the leaderboard, guilds and the transfer code) rests on Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR: it is needed to provide the game to you.
  • Error reports, reports of names, server logs and the checks that keep scores fair rest on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: my legitimate interest in a game that works, is safe to play and cannot easily be cheated.

The game makes no automated decisions that have a legal or similarly significant effect on you.

How long data is kept

  • Account, mage name, progress, score, transfer code: until you delete your account.
  • Guild data: while the guild exists. Leaving a guild removes your membership, the activity feed keeps only the last 14 days, and a guild is deleted with all its raids, quests and feed when its last member leaves.
  • Reports: until the account that made them is deleted; reports about a mage name also go when that player's account is deleted.
  • Error reports: 30 days.

Deleting your data

In the game: Settings → Delete account. This deletes your account and everything stored under it at once, on the device and online.

Without the game: write to mage.clicker0@gmail.com. The page Delete your account explains both ways and lists exactly what is deleted.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to ask what data is stored about you and to get a copy of it, to have wrong data corrected, to have your data deleted or its use restricted, to receive your data in a portable format, and to object to processing that rests on legitimate interests. To use any of these rights, write to mage.clicker0@gmail.com. Because accounts are anonymous, I need your mage name or your transfer code to find your data.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority, for example the one where you live.

Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and older and does not knowingly collect personal data from younger children. If you believe a child has given personal data to the game, for example in a mage name, write to me and I will delete it.
