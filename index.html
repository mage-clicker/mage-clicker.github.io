Mage Clicker

Tap. Ascend. Rule the Tower.

Mage Clicker is an idle game with hands-on spellcasting for Android. Tap a living mana crystal, raise a workshop of apprentices and arcane engines, fight in the Dungeon by casting spells with your own hands, collect gear and rise through nine circles of Ascension.
